# Lecture 2 — A/B Testing (SAMPLE SLIDES)

## Slide 2: What is an A/B test?
An A/B test randomly splits customers into two groups. Group A sees the current version and group B sees a new version. Random assignment means differences in outcomes can be attributed to the change.

## Slide 3: Control and treatment
The control group receives the existing experience. The treatment group receives the change being tested. Both groups should be measured over the same time period.

## Slide 4: Statistical significance
A result is statistically significant when the observed difference is unlikely to be due to chance alone. A common threshold is a p-value below 0.05. Statistical significance does not mean the effect is large or important for the business.
