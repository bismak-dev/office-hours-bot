## Course description
This sample course introduces data-driven decision making for managers. Students learn to frame business questions, summarize data, run simple experiments, and communicate results.

## Instructor and office hours
The instructor is Professor Rivera. Office hours are Tuesdays 2:00-4:00 pm in Room 210. Teaching assistant office hours are Thursdays 10:00-11:00 am on Zoom.

## Grading
Grades are weighted as follows: homework 30%, midterm exam 25%, final project 35%, participation 10%.

## Late policy
Homework submitted up to 24 hours late loses 10% of its points. Homework more than 24 hours late receives no credit unless an extension was approved in advance.

## Key deadlines
Homework is due every Friday at 11:59 pm. The midterm exam is on October 21. The final project report is due December 9 at 5:00 pm.

## AI use policy
Students may use AI tools to study and to explain concepts. Students may not submit AI-generated text as their own work on homework, exams, or the final project.
