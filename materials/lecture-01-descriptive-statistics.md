# Lecture 1 — Descriptive Statistics (SAMPLE SLIDES)

## Slide 2: Mean, median, and mode
The mean is the sum of all values divided by the number of values. The median is the middle value when the data are sorted. The mode is the most frequent value. When data are skewed, the median is often a better summary of a typical value than the mean.

## Slide 3: Measuring spread
The range is the largest value minus the smallest value. The standard deviation measures how far values typically fall from the mean. A larger standard deviation means the data are more spread out.

## Slide 4: Outliers
An outlier is a value far from the rest of the data. Outliers can pull the mean strongly in their direction, while the median is barely affected.
